# Commercial validation plan

## First design partner

Target an MSP or a company spending $50,000–$1 million monthly across at least two of cloud, Kubernetes, SaaS, or GPU infrastructure. Begin with read-only exports and a signed definition of addressable spend.

## Four-week engagement

1. Reconcile one closed invoice and establish allocation coverage.
2. Run anomalies and contract-rate checks; Finance labels true/false positives.
3. Approve at most three reversible changes with explicit SLO gates.
4. Observe the agreed window and sign a realized-savings statement.

## Evidence required for a public case study

- Starting spend and addressable scope, preferably expressed as normalized percentages.
- Exact calculation and exclusions.
- Finding precision after human review.
- Approved and rejected recommendations.
- Net saving after implementation cost.
- Reliability outcome and rollback status.
- Written customer permission for every disclosed fact.

The repository must never label fixture output, list-price comparisons, or projected savings as customer ROI.
